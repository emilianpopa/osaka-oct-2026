Gym practicalities in Japan

  • No booking needed. Walk in, show your passport for the ID check, pay.
  • Ask for the Traveler’s Passport on your first visit: ¥11,000 for 10 days, valid at any Gold’s Gym in Japan. Against ¥2,800 a day that is ¥11,000 instead of ¥22,400 over eight days, and it covers Kyoto too. Not every branch sells it, so ask at the first one.
  • Ask for a free Visitor Card so you skip the paperwork on later visits.
  • Bring a second clean pair of indoor trainers. Mandatory, and the thing that catches visitors out.
  • ClassPass has zero inventory in Osaka. Both classpass.com and classpass.co.jp return no results. Do not rely on it here.
